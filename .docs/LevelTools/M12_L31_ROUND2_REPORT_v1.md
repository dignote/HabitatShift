## Direct-harvest classification

The diagnostic uses legal integer-anchor flood fill with all other Habitats fixed at their initial footprints, foreign Sproutlings and the obstacle as blockers. Distance is Euclidean cell-center distance from the starting footprint. The “immediate” count is matching targets coverable at the start or one adjacent legal anchor. “Simple” is matching supply coverable on straight axis rays without moving a neighbor. `EASY_DIRECT` means simple paths cover more than half the current stage need; `INTERLOCKED` means the stage cannot cover its full need while neighbors stay fixed; otherwise `ROUTED`. Pink takes its upper right and upper left stage-1 targets, then travels west for White. Pink must vacate the White entry once to release the second queued Sproutling.
